Just wrapped an incredible AI enablement workshop with our Solutions Architecture team here in Austin, and I couldn't be prouder of what we accomplished.

One of the key takeaways: there are two types of AI tools we're building and using every day at Simon.

**The Cyborg**: An AI assistant that works with you—augmenting your work, automating repetitive tasks, and acting on your behalf. You're in the driver's seat, just moving faster. We've been using Claude Desktop and a laundry list of MCP servers to build these.

**The Autobot**: An autonomous agent that can act on its own, directed by your goals but capable of executing complex tasks independently. We've been using [PydanticAI](https://ai.pydantic.dev/) and a more select, per-agent list of MCP servers to build these.

We've started using Cyborgs to help automate away repetitive tasks like time tracking, while we're using Autobots to search, unify, and summarize fractured knowledge across the enterprise.

Big thanks to the team for the energy, curiosity, and openness to experimentation. I'm really looking forward to seeing where Solutions Architecture takes our AI tooling.
