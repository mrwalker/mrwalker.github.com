At Simon Data, we're leveraging Snowflake Cortex as the command center for our production AI automation strategy.

Our AI ecosystem has distinct components:

* Cyborgs (AI assistants) that help our team internally
* Internal automation robots ("Autobots") that automate our business processes
* Production Autobots that power user-facing features with customer data

It's specifically this third category—our production Autobots—that rely on Snowflake Cortex as their secure home base, because these automation agents need direct, governed access to sensitive customer data.

What makes Cortex the ideal platform for these production Autobots?

* It houses a well-stocked arsenal of foundation models from Anthropic, Meta, and others – giving our Autobots access to the right LLM for any customer-facing mission
* Customer data remains secure and governed, removing significant compliance headaches
* The platform scales effortlessly to process massive amounts of customer data
* Its developer-focused approach has accelerated our deployment cycles

For companies building production-grade AI applications that require customer data access, finding the right infrastructure is critical. Cortex has been our answer for the most sensitive part of our AI portfolio.
