I'm not a DevOps engineer, but I play one on TV... and during on-call rotations. 🦇

Jake's post about DevOps engineers being Batman (not Superman) deeply resonated with me, in particular his insight about the sheer amount of context these folks have to juggle at once.

As someone who context-switches between being Co-founder, CTO, CISO, senior engineer, and sometimes DevOps engineer at Simon AI, I'm all too familiar with the mental load of keeping dozens of systems, dependencies, and edge cases in working memory. The difference between being effective and being overwhelmed often comes down to having the right tools *and knowing how to wield them*.

Jake nails the shift we're experiencing: from the "latency era" (how fast can we ship?) to the "bandwidth era" (how much can we ship?). AI isn't giving us superpowers, but it's expanding our utility belt. Like Batman's grappling hook, it's only game-changing if you practice using it to master it.
