🚀 Today marks a pivotal moment: Simon Data becomes Simon AI, and we're launching [simon.ai](http://simon.ai)

Jason and I have spent over 10 years working to democratize data for marketers. We've built industry-leading, scalable solutions that deliver real value. But standing next to Mexico City's Monument to Independence during our recent company offsite, I realized Simon AI is our own monument to what's now possible for marketers.

For too long, the most sophisticated marketing personalization has been locked behind technical barriers. Even with everything we've built, marketers without deep technical expertise still struggle to unlock their data's full potential.

Simon AI changes that equation entirely.

We're not just rebranding - we're declaring independence from the status quo. AI provides the flexibility and automation to finally free marketers from data plumbing, letting them focus on what they do best: creative, impactful customer engagement.

Every Simon AI user now has an army of intelligent agents at their disposal, filling gaps in capacity, expertise, and execution. The most effective marketing can now reach the widest range of customers, overcoming technical barriers.

A big part of this future is here today at [simon.ai](http://simon.ai)
