A few months ago, I shared our vision for "composable agents" - the next evolution in our AI strategy at Simon Data.

**Today, that vision is live.**

We've launched Simon's Composable AI Agents, built natively on **Snowflake** Cortex and powered by **Anthropic** Claude.

What we've built extends our data composability principles directly to AI agents themselves:

🔒 **Data never leaves the client's Snowflake environment** - no ETL, no data movement, zero security compromises

🏛️ **LLMs execute within customer governance perimeter** - same RBAC, audit trails, compliance frameworks that already protect their data

⚡ **Row-level LLM operations at enterprise scale** - process millions of customer records with ease

But here's what excites me most as an engineer: we went from concept to production in months, not years. Composable architectures don't just make better products - they make teams move faster.

Our three agent types that work in concert:

📊 **Insights Agents** - Surface hidden patterns using Claude's advanced reasoning

🧬 **Data Agents** - Transform unstructured context into actionable "Smart Fields"

🤖 **Automation Agents** - Execute hundreds of adaptive campaigns automatically

Huge gratitude to the **Snowflake** team, especially Ed DiCosmo, Aric Wang, and Alejandro Herrera, for the partnership that made this architecture possible. Cortex provided exactly what we needed: enterprise AI without architectural compromises.

Incredibly proud of our engineering team for building something that doesn't just add AI to existing workflows - it creates an entirely new operating model for enterprise AI. I'll be sharing the technical deep dive soon.
