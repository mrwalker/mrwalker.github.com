I'm excited to share insights from our VP of Engineering, Ernesto Gonzalez, on how we've built something fundamentally different in the AI-for-marketing space.

For over 10 years, Jason and I have worked to democratize data for marketers. We've built industry-leading solutions, but I've watched too many "AI marketing" solutions fall short because they're fundamentally bolt-on point solutions.

**Ernesto nails why our approach has been so successful.**

Instead of layering AI recommendations on top of static workflows, we've embedded agentic AI into every step of the data-to-execution process — running directly inside your cloud data warehouse with zero data movement.

This architectural choice is why our composable agents have been so successful:

✅ **Full data universe access** — not just customer profiles, but operational, contextual, and real-world signals

✅ **Rich semantic layer** — translating raw warehouse data into marketing-ready concepts

✅ **Native execution tools** — agents that can act directly within the CDP, no handoffs required

Ernesto's deep dive shows exactly why that foundation matters in AI. When agents have access to your complete data picture and can act natively within your infrastructure, personalization finally scales without the traditional trade-offs.
